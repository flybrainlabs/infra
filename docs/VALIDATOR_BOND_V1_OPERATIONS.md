## Admission and rollout

Deploy the separate bond program, verify its ELF and initialized policy, then
fund each exact scientific owner address and deposit exactly 100,000 FBW into its
derived vault. Every owner signs its own deposit with its existing private seed;
the seeds stay on their respective hosts. The project payer can pay account rent.

Install the reviewed worker package and staking deployment configuration only
after the corresponding finalized deposit is verified. Production file loading
requires staking configuration. Workers fail startup without a valid active bond,
recheck it on health and admission, and persist admission records before native
work. The coordinator separately checks each bond before dispatch and verifies
that the signed report binds the expected bond and generation. Fixtures may
construct an in-memory legacy fleet without staking; deployed file loading cannot.

Use the existing authenticated read-only chain relay. Do not publish personal
RPC keys, open worker ports or change frozen settlement signers and identities.
Install one worker at a time; preserve reports, model files, output directories,
databases, raw transactions and prior releases. Recheck at least three healthy
bonded participants before enabling the updated coordinator. No validator count
here defines a fixed network membership or independent operators.

## Exit and responsibility clearance

1. Use the owner to request exit only after 30 complete chain days. Confirm it at
   finalized commitment. Exiting participants must receive no new assignments.
2. Drain admitted native jobs. Preserve all bond-admissions journals and signed
   reports; a reported result is not itself a settled or cleared responsibility.
3. Audit every replay admission in the exiting generation against its frozen
   task, submission, final settlement/expiry and applicable dispute procedure.
   Reconcile running/unresolved records after crashes and outstanding signed
   packets. Self-checks are not public scientific obligations.
4. Extend the wait if the actual task, evidence, appeal or finality window requires
   it. Seven days is a minimum, not a claim that all future windows fit seven days.
5. Record a durable clearance document and its hash. Only after all liabilities
   close and the chain withdrawal time arrives may the clearance authority and
   owner co-sign Withdraw for the current generation and owner's destination.

Withdrawal requires trusted clearance review because historical V4 tasks do not
maintain on-chain collateral liabilities. No automated clearance or arbitrary
slashing is enabled. Do not issue a clearance merely because health says idle.
Back up the validator owner and project clearance identities privately. Key loss
or unavailable review can delay access to principal. Never run an early public
withdrawal test by weakening the fixed time constants.
